CALC · Chart Guide

Basic

  • Bar — scores per course, side by side
  • Horizontal Bar — same, rotated for long names
  • Line — score trend across courses
  • Pie — score share per course
  • Doughnut — pie with centre callout

Advanced

  • Scatter — score vs credit unit or grade point
  • Bubble — 3-axis: CU × score × credit point
  • Radar / Spider — balanced subject profile
  • Polar Area — radial score distribution
  • Area — filled line with trend overlay

Comparison

  • Stacked Bar — same courses across semesters
  • Multi-Semester Line — overlay all semesters
  • GPA Summary Bar — one bar per semester GPA

Scale note: Credit Point, Grade Point, GPA, and CGPA values follow the grading scale selected above. Choose 4.0 or 5.0 before generating a chart.

Degree Classification by Scale

  • Pass 1.00 – 1.49
