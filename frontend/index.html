Understand the basics

Key Concepts You Need to Know

Before calculating, make sure you understand these four building blocks of your academic score.

Credit Unit

A standardised measure of a course's academic workload. Typically 1–4 units per course, representing weekly contact hours plus out-of-class study time.

Example Mathematics — 3 credit units

Score & Grade

Your raw exam score (0–100) maps to a letter grade. Select the grading scale used by your school on the calculator page.

Example Score 78 → Grade A → 4.0 or 5.0 CP

Credit Point

The numerical weight assigned to each grade. It ranges from 0.0 (F) to either 4.0 or 5.0 (A), depending on your selected school scale.

Scale 4.0 scale: A=4.0 · AB=3.5 · B=3.25

Grade Point

Credit Unit × Credit Point for each course. Sum all grade points then divide by total credit units to get your GPA.

Formula 3 CU × 3.5 CP = 10.5 GP

Step by step

How to Use the Calculator

Follow these steps on the calculator page to get accurate results.

  1. 01

    Enter Your Courses

    Type each course name in the Course column. Add as many courses as you have in that semester using the Add Course button.

  2. 02

    Enter Credit Units & Scores

    Input the credit unit for each course and your exam score (0–100). The calculator will automatically determine your grade and credit point.

  3. 03

    Add More Semesters

    Click Add Semester to create additional semester tables. Switch between semesters using the dropdown selector.

  4. 04

    Calculate GPA & CGPA

    Click Calculate GPA to see each semester's result, then Calculate CGPA for your cumulative average across all semesters.

  5. 05

    View Chart Analysis

    Select your school's 4.0 or 5.0 grading scale, then choose Bar, Line, or Pie chart to see a visual breakdown of your scores for any semester. Credit Point and Grade Point charts use that selected scale.

  6. 06

    Export Your Results

    Use the Export to PDF or Export CSV buttons to save your results for your records or share with an adviser.
