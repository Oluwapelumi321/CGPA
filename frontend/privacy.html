Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to CALC ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the CALC GPA & CGPA Calculator website. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

2. Data We Collect

We do not receive personal data on our servers. The CALC calculator operates in your browser. Course names, scores, credit units, calculation history, reviews, and your selected grading scale are processed and stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to our server.

  • No account registration is required.
  • No cookies are set by this website.
  • No tracking pixels or analytics scripts are embedded.
  • Calculation history, reviews, and preferences use your browser's local storage.
  • You can remove local calculation data using the calculator's reset and history controls, or by clearing site data in your browser.
  • No calculation data is stored on our servers.

3. Third-Party Services

This website loads resources from the following third-party CDNs solely to render the interface:

  • Google Fonts — typography (Inter, Syne). Google may log your IP when fonts are requested. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • cdnjs.cloudflare.com — Font Awesome icons. Cloudflare may log requests. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.
  • cdn.jsdelivr.net — Chart.js library. jsDelivr may log requests. See jsDelivr's Privacy Policy.

We do not control these third parties and are not responsible for their data practices.

4. Security

Even though we collect no data, the website is served over HTTPS with strict security headers including Content Security Policy, HSTS, and X-Frame-Options to protect you from common web threats.

5. Children's Privacy

This website does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since no data is collected at all, COPPA and similar regulations do not apply.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes. Continued use of the website after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
